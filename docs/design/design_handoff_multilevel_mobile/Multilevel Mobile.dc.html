B1 Grammatika
Present Perfect vs Past Simple
{{ stepLabel }}
NAZARIYA

Ikki o'tgan zamon, ikki xil maqsad

Past Simple — ish-harakat qachon bo'lganini bilamiz va u tugagan. Vaqt aniq: yesterday, in 2019, two days ago, last week.

Present Perfect — harakat o'tgan zamonda bo'lgan, lekin muhimi natija hozir. Aniq vaqt aytilmaydi.

  • Tajriba: I have been to Samarkand.
  • Hozirgacha davom etayotgan holat: She has worked here for 3 years.
  • Yaqinda bo'lgan va natijasi ko'rinib turgan ish: I have lost my keys.

Imtihonda eng ko'p uchraydigan xato — Present Perfect'ni aniq vaqt bilan ishlatish. Buni eslab qolsangiz, Writing'da bir nechta ball tejaysiz.
MISOLLAR
I finished my homework an hour ago.
Uy vazifamni bir soat oldin tugatdim.
I have finished my homework.
Uy vazifamni tugatdim. (Endi bo'shman — natija hozir muhim.)
Have you ever taken the multilevel exam?
Hech multilevel imtihonini topshirganmisiz?
JADVAL

Vaqt ifodalari qaysi zaman bilan keladi

VAQT IFODASI ZAMON MISOL
yesterday, ago Past Simple He called me yesterday.
ever, never Present Perfect I have never tried it.
for, since Present Perfect We have known each other since 2020.
just, already, yet Present Perfect She has just left.
← jadvalni yon tomonga suring
DIALOG
A
Have you finished the reading section?
Reading bo'limini tugatdingizmi?
B
Yes, I have. I finished it twenty minutes ago.
Ha, tugatdim. Yigirma daqiqa oldin tugatdim.
A
Was it difficult?
Qiyin bo'ldimi?
B
Not really. I have done six mocks, so I'm used to it now.
Unchalik emas. Oltita mock yechganman, endi o'rganib qolganman.
E'tibor bering: twenty minutes ago — Past Simple, I have done six mocks — vaqt aytilmagan, natija muhim.
MASLAHAT
Bitta savol bering
Gapni yozishdan oldin o'zingizdan so'rang: "Qachon bo'lgani muhimmi?" Agar javob "ha" bo'lsa — Past Simple. Agar "yo'q, natijasi muhim" bo'lsa — Present Perfect.
